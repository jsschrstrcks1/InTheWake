## Getting to the ship and getting home

### Sunday arrival, Monday boarding

**Sunday, November 29: fly in.** It's the Sunday after Thanksgiving, so book early.

**Monday, November 30: boarding.**

- **Terminal V**, 718 N. Cruise Blvd. For GPS, Virgin says to enter 1015 North America Way, Miami, FL 33132.
- **Parking:** PortMiami's Garage G, $25 a day (PortMiami, October 2026).
- **Arrival:** pick a terminal arrival time in the app and don't come earlier. Luggage drop opens at 9:00 AM.
- **From the airport:** about 9 miles. Taxis are metered, $15 minimum.
- **Carry-on:** passport, medications, swimsuit, change of clothes, charger.

### Thursday, December 10: home

- The ship is due in at 6:30 AM. Allow up to an hour for the gangway to open.
- Keep your passport, medications and a change of clothes with you.

## Areas of interest during the cruise

### On the ship

- **Rojo by Razzle Dazzle:** tapas, on Brilliant Lady only.
- **Every restaurant is included,** except a few "Treat Yourself" items and the paid dinner shows (Another Rose and It's a Ship Show).
- **[The Galley](/restaurants/virgin/the-galley.html):** the food hall. An easy place to eat alone.
- **Soda and water are included.** Drinks are pay-as-you-go or a pre-paid Bar Tab. There are no drink packages.
- **Solo meetups:** Virgin runs an Evening Meet-Up, a Morning Meet-Up and a "Let's Set Sail" event. Check the app.
- **Scarlet Night:** Virgin's red-themed party night. Pack something red if you like.

### Port-day picks (one each)

- **St. Maarten:** Great Bay beach and the boardwalk, walking distance from the port and free.
- **Tortola:** Cane Garden Bay ($10 to $16 per person by taxi each way, per a 2026 travel guide).
- **St. Croix:** Fort Frederik and a walk around Frederiksted.
- **San Juan:** El Morro. Bring a card, and be back by 4:00 PM.
- **Puerto Plata:** the old town and Fortaleza San Felipe.
- **Bimini:** a beach day at the Beach Club, all included except cocktails.

### The group

Tina's flyer promises a host just for the group, group dining each night, daily meetups, optional group shore excursions, a private group chat, and games and surprises. Nothing is required. Go to the first dinner and see.
