## What the at-home contact does if a day or two passes without word

1. **Don't panic.** Ship Wi-Fi is patchy. Silence usually means no signal, not an emergency.
2. **Call Tina** at Maulsby Travel Co. (910-528-5077). She booked the cruise and can reach the group's host.
3. **To get a message to the ship,** call NCL's line above. It's a paid call.
4. **For a real emergency, call the State Department's Overseas Citizens Services.** It's free and runs around the clock.
5. **To see where the ship is,** use cruisinginthewake.com/tools/ship-tracker.html.
