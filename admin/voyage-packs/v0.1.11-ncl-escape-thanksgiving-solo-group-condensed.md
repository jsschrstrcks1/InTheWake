**Documents:** for this round trip NCL accepts a passport, or a state-certified US birth certificate with a government photo ID. NCL "strongly recommends" a passport.

**Service charge:** $20 per person per day in standard cabins, $25 in suites and The Haven (NCL, October 2026). That's $140 for seven nights in a standard cabin. Ask Tina whether your group rate prepaid it.

**Weather, in one line:** NOAA's normals for these dates are a 76°F high and 59°F low near Port Canaveral, and 86°F and 76°F at St. Thomas, with a shower on about half the St. Thomas days. Not a forecast. Hurricane season officially ends November 30.

**A just-refreshed ship.** NCL says Escape gets a refresh in late October 2026: an expanded Vibe Beach Club, a new Palomar restaurant, a Starbucks in the Atrium and a pickleball court. Check the app for what's open.

## Getting to the ship and getting home

### Thursday arrival, Friday boarding

**Thursday, November 19: fly in.** A day early means a late flight doesn't cost you the cruise.

**Friday, November 20: boarding.**

- **Terminal:** NCL's Port Canaveral page says it "typically sails out of Cruise Terminal 5 (CT5) and Cruise Terminal 6 (CT6), but this is subject to change." Your eDocs name yours.
- **Parking:** "$20.00 per day plus tax," counting the day you arrive and the day you leave (Port Canaveral). Paid when you drive in, by credit card only. No reservations.
- **Arrival:** if you've checked in online, arrive no later than one hour before the boarding time you chose. Be aboard by 3:00 PM.
- **Carry-on:** ID documents, medications, swimsuit, change of clothes, charger.
- **Sailaway with the group:** Pam posts the meeting spot in the Facebook chat.

### Friday, November 27: home

- The ship is due in at 7:00 AM.
- Keep your ID, medications and a change of clothes with you.
- It's Black Friday. Leave extra time on the roads.

## Areas of interest during the cruise

### On the ship

- **Included restaurants:** the [Manhattan Room](/restaurants/ncl/the-manhattan-room.html) (Deck 7), Taste and Savor (Deck 6), the [Garden Café](/restaurants/ncl/garden-cafe.html) buffet (Deck 16), the American Diner (Deck 17) and [O'Sheehan's](/restaurants/ncl/osheehans.html) (Deck 7).
- **Specialty restaurants:** Cagney's, Food Republic, La Cucina, Bayamo by Ocean Blue, Moderno, Pincho Tapas Bar, Teppanyaki and Le Bistro. $40 to $60 cover, plus 20%, beyond your Free at Sea meals. Palomar joins after the refresh.
- **Free at Sea** (bookings from November 5, 2025): open bar for 21 and over, **three specialty meals on a 7-night cruise**, 150 Wi-Fi minutes, and a $50 excursion credit per port for the first guest. The open bar carries $28.50 a day in gratuities (NCL's pre-cruise price), about $200 for seven nights. Your group rate may differ; ask Tina.
- **More Wi-Fi:** $29.99 a day per cabin, or $39.99 for streaming.
- **Outdoors:** the Ropes Course (Deck 18), the Aqua Park (Deck 16), and The Waterfront promenade (Deck 8).
- **Paid extras:** Vibe Beach Club (adults only) and the spa's Thermal Suite.
- **Solo cabins:** Studios and Solo Inside, Oceanview and Balcony cabins, all with access to the **[Studio Lounge](/restaurants/ncl/studio-lounge.html)** on Deck 11 (coffee, espresso and snacks, per NCL).

### Port-day picks (one each)

- **Puerto Plata:** an NCL Shore Excursion, or an easy day at the port.
- **St. Thomas:** Charlotte Amalie, or Magens Bay (an entry fee for visitors; $7 from October 2023, per VI Consortium).
- **Tortola:** a morning in Road Town. Back by 1:00 PM.
- **Great Stirrup Cay:** a beach and a lounger, free. Leave time to rest before Thanksgiving dinner.

### Thanksgiving

NCL's Thanksgiving page promises "festive menus without the stress," and publishes no menu or times. Check the daily schedule in the app that morning. Pam's plan for the group's holiday dinner will be in the Facebook chat. If the day is hard, that's allowed; you don't have to perform a celebration.

### Sailing solo

Pick one plan a day, eat where it's easy, and send one message home each evening. Fill in the handoff card before you go: it's a separate one-page document, and the full pack has it too.
