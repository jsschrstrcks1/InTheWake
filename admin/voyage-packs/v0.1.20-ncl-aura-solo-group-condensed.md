## Getting to the ship and getting home

### Friday arrival, Saturday boarding

**Friday, October 13: fly in.** The ship leaves at 4:00 PM, and a same-day flight leaves no margin.

**Saturday, October 14: boarding.**

- **Terminal:** NCL's PortMiami terminal, 1015 N. America Way. NCL says to enter **1761 N. Cruise Blvd.** in your GPS and park in **Garage B**. Your boarding documents name the terminal; check them.
- **Online check-in** in the NCL app before you leave home.
- **Carry-on:** passport, medications, swimsuit, change of clothes, charger.

### Saturday, October 21: home

- The ship is due in at 7:00 AM. NCL tells you the night before how bags and walk-off work.
- Keep your passport, medications and a change of clothes with you.

## Areas of interest during the cruise

### On the ship (as NCL has announced it)

- **Ocean Heights**, Decks 18 to 21: Eclipse Racers, Aura Free Fall, The Wave, The Drop (Deck 18 to Deck 8), ropes course, climbing wall, mini golf. Check the app for any fees.
- **Ocean Boulevard**, Deck 8: the outdoor walkway with hot tubs and a bar.
- **[Studio Lounge](/restaurants/ncl/studio-lounge.html)**: for Solo Studio guests only. Where solo cruisers find each other.
- **[Indulge Food Hall](/restaurants/ncl/indulge-food-hall.html)**: nine counters, easy for eating alone.
- **Shows:** *Whitney: A Celebration of Whitney Houston* and others. LunaTique is adults-only and costs extra.

### Specialty dining

New on Aura: **Tamara**, NCL's first Indian restaurant (Deck 8). Also [Cagney's](/restaurants/ncl/cagneys-steakhouse.html), [Le Bistro](/restaurants/ncl/le-bistro.html), [Sukhothai](/restaurants/ncl/sukhothai.html), [Palomar](/restaurants/ncl/palomar.html), [Onda by Scarpetta](/restaurants/ncl/onda-by-scarpetta.html), [Hasuki](/restaurants/ncl/hasuki.html) and [Nama](/restaurants/ncl/nama-sushi.html). Book when NCL opens reservations.

### Port-day picks (one each)

- **Puerto Plata:** the old town and Fortaleza San Felipe, if you dock at Taino Bay. Or NCL's Damajagua Waterfalls tour (from $119.99 in 2026; age 8 and up; closed-toe shoes).
- **St. Thomas:** Magens Bay. Taxis charge per person ($15 one person, $12 each for two or more, Havensight to Magens Bay, on a 2024 rate card).
- **Tortola:** Cane Garden Bay ($10 to $16 per person by taxi each way, per a 2026 travel guide). Back by 1:00 PM.
- **Great Stirrup Cay:** a beach, lunch and the pool, all included.

### The group

The flyer promises a dedicated host, daily group dining, group shore excursion options, meetups and a private group chat. Nothing is required. Go to one dinner and see.
