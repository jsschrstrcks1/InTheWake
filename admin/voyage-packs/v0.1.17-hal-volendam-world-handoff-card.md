## What the at-home contact does if the quiet stretches too long

1. **Check the agreed rhythm first** — on this voyage, multi-day silence is normal at sea. The Antarctic days, the Pacific crossing, and the Indian Ocean legs are the long-quiet stretches; the route block above says roughly where the ship is by month.
2. **Track the ship** at /tools/ship-tracker.html or marinetraffic.com for context — moving ship, normal quiet.
3. **Call Holland America** — +1 800-426-0327 in Pacific business hours, +1 800-628-4771 after hours and holidays; they confirm the cruiser boarded and relay messages to the ship anywhere on earth.
4. **If HAL confirms an incident abroad:** the U.S. State Department line (+1 888-407-4747) routes to the right embassy or consulate for whichever country the ship is in — that's exactly what it's for. For a medical situation aboard, the ship's medical center acts first; the evacuation-policy number above is the one the insurer needs.
