## What the at-home contact does if 36+ hours pass without word

1. **Don't panic** — cruise Wi-Fi is unreliable; silence usually means signal, not emergency.
2. **Call MSC Guest Relations** above; they'll confirm boarding and relay messages.
3. **Reach the travel agent** listed above for booking questions or if Guest Relations isn't getting through.
4. **For a true emergency, call the State Department's Overseas Citizens Services line** — 24/7, locates the ship, no cost.
5. **If MSC confirms an incident:** US Consulate Tegucigalpa (Honduras); US Consulate Mérida (Mexico); ship resources (Ocean Cay). Track ship position at /tools/ship-tracker.html.

*Soli Deo Gloria.*
