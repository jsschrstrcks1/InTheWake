1. **Don't panic.** Ship Wi-Fi drops, and people forget.
2. **Wait for the next check-in,** then try phones and messages.
3. **Call Inspiration Travel** at 800-247-1899.
4. **Call Holland America's emergency line** above.
5. **For a real emergency, call the State Department** at +1 (888) 407-4747.
6. **To see where the ship is,** use cruisinginthewake.com/tools/ship-tracker.html.
