**Documents:** for this round trip NCL accepts a passport, or a state-certified US birth certificate with a government photo ID. NCL "strongly recommends" a passport.

**Service charge:** $20 per person per day in standard cabins, $25 in suites and The Haven (NCL, October 2026). That's $240 for twelve nights in a standard cabin. Ask Tina whether your group rate includes it.

**Weather, in one line:** five years of records for these dates average highs of 80°F in Miami to 91°F in Cartagena, with rain on most days at Puerto Limón and about half at the canal. Not a forecast. Bring a rain jacket for Days 9 and 10.

**The ship's future.** NCL's parent company said in April 2025 that Norwegian Sun will be chartered to Cordelia Cruises, starting in 2027. Your March sailing was on sale in October 2026; NCL and Tina will tell you if anything changes.

## Getting to the ship and getting home

### Sunday arrival, Monday boarding

**Sunday, February 28: fly in.** A day early means a late flight doesn't cost you the cruise.

**Monday, March 1: boarding.**

- **Terminal:** PortMiami lists Terminal B, "The Pearl of Miami," as NCL's terminal, and Terminal C as shared. Your eDocs name yours.
- **Parking:** Garage B, 1756 N. Cruise Blvd., $35 a day; Garage C, $28 (PortMiami, effective January 1, 2025). No debit cards.
- **Arrival:** if you've checked in online, arrive no later than one hour before the boarding time you chose. Be aboard by 3:00 PM.
- **Carry-on:** passport, medications, swimsuit, change of clothes, charger.
- **Sailaway with the group:** Tina will say where to meet.

### Saturday, March 13: home

- The ship is due in at 7:00 AM.
- Keep your passport, medications and a change of clothes with you.

## Areas of interest during the cruise

### On the ship

- **Included restaurants:** Four Seasons and Seven Seas (Deck 5), the [Garden Café](/restaurants/ncl/garden-cafe.html) buffet and [The Great Outdoors](/restaurants/ncl/the-great-outdoors.html) (Deck 11), and the Sports Bar (Deck 11, 24 hours).
- **Specialty restaurants:** [Cagney's](/restaurants/ncl/cagneys-steakhouse.html), [Teppanyaki](/restaurants/ncl/teppanyaki.html), [Le Bistro](/restaurants/ncl/le-bistro.html), [Los Lobos](/restaurants/ncl/los-lobos.html), the Sushi Bar, [La Cucina](/restaurants/ncl/la-cucina.html) and [Moderno](/restaurants/ncl/moderno-churrascaria.html). $40 to $60 cover, plus 20%, beyond your Free at Sea meals.
- **Free at Sea** (bookings from November 5, 2025): open bar for 21 and over, **four specialty meals on a cruise of nine nights or more**, 150 Wi-Fi minutes, and a $50 excursion credit per port for the first guest. The open bar carries $28.50 a day in gratuities (NCL's pre-cruise price), $342 for twelve nights. Your group rate may differ; ask Tina.
- **More Wi-Fi:** $29.99 a day per cabin, or $39.99 for streaming.
- **Shows:** the Stardust Theater (Decks 6 and 7).
- **Solo cabins:** Solo Inside, Oceanview and Balcony. Sun has no Studio cabins or Studio Lounge; Tina's group fills that gap.

### Port-day picks (one each)

- **Great Stirrup Cay:** a beach and a lounger, free.
- **Cabo Rojo:** an NCL Shore Excursion to Bahía de las Águilas, or an easy day at the port.
- **Aruba:** downtown Oranjestad and a beach.
- **Curaçao:** Willemstad on foot, across the Queen Emma Bridge.
- **Cartagena:** the walled city, with the group or an excursion. Back by 1:00 PM.
- **The canal:** a spot on the open deck at 5:00 AM.
- **Colón:** a short walk near the terminal, or stay aboard.
- **Puerto Limón:** look for sloths in port. Back by 12:30 PM.

Tina is on board with the group. Pick one plan a day, eat where it's easy, and send one message home each evening. Fill in the handoff card before you go: it's a separate one-page document, and the full pack has it too.
