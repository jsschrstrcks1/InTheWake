## Getting to the ship and getting home

- **Fly into Barcelona a day early.** Inspiration Travel offers flight and hotel quotes and pier transfers.
- **Fly home from Athens.** Holland America hasn't published the arrival time yet, and it will tell you when you can leave the ship. Don't book a tight flight.
- **Passports.** The State Department recommends six months of validity for Greece, and Turkey requires passports "valid for six months beyond your entry date." Make sure yours runs well past March 2028.
- **No visa needed** for US citizens on this trip, per the State Department: up to 90 days in Spain, Malta and Greece (Schengen), and up to 90 days in any 180 in Turkey.

## Money and what costs extra

- **Fare:** the brochure's "starting at $2,958" a person, two to a cabin, includes cruise fare and government taxes and fees. **Air isn't included.** $50 off a person for booking online.
- **Crew Appreciation:** $18 a person a day in a standard cabin, $20 in a suite (Holland America FAQ). About **$126 a person** for the week. Ask Inspiration Travel if your package includes it.
- **Aboard:** US dollars. **Ashore:** euro in Spain, Malta and Greece. Turkey isn't on the euro; cards work at most tourist places.
- **Extra:** drinks, specialty dining, spa, Wi-Fi, laundry (no self-service), other excursions.

## Medicare: read this twice

Medicare's own guide: "Medicare doesn't cover health care services you get when the ship is more than 6 hours away from a U.S. port," and "Medicare plans can't cover drugs you buy outside the U.S." **Ask about a travel medical policy, with medical evacuation, before you go.** The ship's medical center has doctors and nurses on call 24/7 for urgent care, and it bills you.

## Getting on and off

Holland America says every ship except Volendam and Zaandam, so Oosterdam too, has an elevator to the tender platform. Gangways can be steep at low or high tide, and the captain decides whether it's safe to help a guest ashore. Tell Inspiration Travel about mobility needs when you book.

## Travel advisories (read November 2026)

Spain Level 2 · Malta Level 1 · Greece Level 1 · Turkey Level 2 (its "do not travel" area is the Syria and Iraq border region). These describe November 2026, not your sailing week. They change both ways. Check again the week before.
