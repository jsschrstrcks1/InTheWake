No Affiliate Links

Last updated: October 4, 2026

The Short Version

In the Wake has no affiliate relationships. When a page links to a product on Amazon or anywhere else, the link goes straight to the store. We earn nothing when you click it, and nothing when you buy.

In the past, some product links used the Amazon Associates program. That has ended, and those links have been replaced with plain ones.

Our Independence

We are not affiliated with any cruise line.

In the Wake has no financial relationship with Royal Caribbean, Carnival, Norwegian, Disney, or any other cruise company. Our reviews, recommendations, and opinions about cruise lines are entirely independent.

We will never accept payment for cruise bookings, shore excursions, or cruise line promotions. Our cruise advice remains uninfluenced by commercial interests.

How We Choose What to Recommend

We recommend products based on:

  • Research — Reading reviews, comparing specifications, checking ratings
  • Personal experience — Products we've used ourselves on cruises
  • Community feedback — What experienced cruisers recommend
  • Value — Products that offer good quality at reasonable prices

We do not choose products based on sponsorship, payment from brands, or pressure from manufacturers. We don't put buy links on health products at all.

Our Commitment to You

In the Wake was built on trust. We started this site to provide honest, helpful cruise guidance — not to make money from product sales.

  • Honesty — We'll tell you when something isn't worth buying
  • Independence — We're not beholden to any cruise line or brand
  • Helpfulness — Every recommendation is meant to make your cruise better
  • Transparency — If that ever changes, this page will say so first

Questions?

If you have questions about a recommendation, please reach out through our Support page. We're happy to explain why we recommend any product, share our research, or point you to alternatives.
