## What the at-home contact does if a check-in is missed

1. **Don't panic.** Ship Wi-Fi drops out. Silence usually means no signal, not an emergency.
2. **Wait for the next check-in,** then try phone and messages.
3. **Call NCL's ship number** above. NCL says it's paid by card.
4. **For a real emergency, call the State Department** at +1 (888) 407-4747. It's free and runs around the clock.
5. **To see where the ship is,** use cruisinginthewake.com/tools/ship-tracker.html.

*Numbers checked October 2026 on each embassy or consulate's own site, NCL's FAQ and the UK government's travel advice. Check them again before you go. Soli Deo Gloria.*
