## Getting to the ship — and getting home

### Arrival: Friday recommended, Saturday required

**Friday, December 4 — fly in.** Same-day flights to an afternoon all-aboard are risky, and December adds front-driven delays across the eastern seaboard. Pre-cruise hotel in Brickell, Downtown Miami, or Miami Beach.

**Saturday, December 5 — embarkation.**

- **Terminal:** **MSC Miami Cruise Terminal** at PortMiami — the MSC-built terminal opened April 2025. *Different from where Royal Caribbean and Carnival board.* Verify the address in the MSC for Me app.
- **MIA → MSC Terminal:** Uber/Lyft $30–45, 20–30 min. Taxi flat-rate $32.
- **FLL → MSC Terminal:** Uber/Lyft $60–90, 45–60 min.
- **If driving:** Terminal garage ran **$35/day** (~$245/week, firsthand May 2026 — verify); offsite lots $7–14/day with shuttle — weigh the shuttle against a family's bag pile.
- **Embark window:** 11:30 AM – 3:30 PM. Published departure ~4:30–5:00 PM (listings differ — the app is authoritative); be aboard with an hour's margin — more with kids.
- **Online check-in via MSC for Me** for every passenger — done at home beats a 20-minute queue with children.

### Day 8 debark and the trip home

- **The night before:** bags in the hall by ~11 PM with the color-coded tag — or self-carry everything and walk off in the first wave (with kids, seriously consider it).
- **6:00 – 8:00 AM:** Breakfast at Il Mercato or La Brasserie.
- **~7:00 AM:** Arrival + customs clearance. Self-disembarkation begins.
- **7:30 – 10:00 AM:** Walk-off in waves. All passengers off by 10:00 AM.

**Carry-on on debark morning:** change of clothes per person, all medications, passports, chargers, valuables.

**PortMiami → MIA:** Uber/Lyft **$30–45, 25–30 minutes**; pre-book against Saturday surge. Domestic flight after 4 PM → leave the port by 1:30 PM; MIA TSA runs 30–60 min in the Saturday return rush.

**Killing hours before an evening flight:** Bayfront Park / Bayside Marketplace (10-min walk) · Pérez Art Museum ($20, air-conditioned) · Brickell by short Uber.

## Areas of interest during the cruise

### On the ship — World America spots worth knowing

- **The World Promenade** (Deck 8 aft) — seven-deck outdoor walkway around the Y-shaped stern. Signature space; December evenings up here want a light layer.
- **The World Galleria** (Decks 6–8 mid-ship) — three-deck LED-ceiling atrium. **The family meeting point:** "if we're separated — Galleria, Deck 6, on the hour."
- **Venom Drop @ The Spiral** — 11-deck dry slide; longest at sea. First hour after opening (typically 10 AM) has the shortest line.
- **Doremiland Kids Clubs / The Harbour** — included; register Day 1 when lines are shortest. Port days run longer morning sessions.
- **Cliffhanger @ The Harbour** — the only over-water swing at sea; four seats, ~160 ft above the water. Check the app for cost/height rules before promising a turn.
- **Aurea Spa thermal suite** — sea-day intro rates; ~90 minutes is the right dose.
- **La Brasserie** (Deck 19) — 24/7 French-style casual; the calm breakfast.
- **Luna Park Pizza & Burger** (Deck 6) — the reliable kid-dinner fallback, included.

### Sea-day priorities (Day 2 + Day 6)

1. **Sunrise coffee at the World Promenade** — kids still asleep, wake unfolding below.
2. **Venom Drop first thing** — empty in the first hour.
3. **Kids-club morning session** — and the quiet hour it buys.
4. **Eataly at lunch** if dinner is booked out.
5. **Port talk for tomorrow's destination** — the Roatán one especially (weather + logistics).

### Specialty dining picks

- **Eataly** (Deck 8) — first Eataly at sea; the one specialty venue that genuinely works for kids. **Book on Day 1.**
- **Kaito Teppanyaki** — the showy hibachi room; a family-dinner win.
- **Paxos** (Deck 8) — Greek seafood on the Promenade.
- **Butcher's Cut** — steakhouse, for the grown-ups' night.

### Port-day picks (one each)

- **Roatán** — West Bay Beach via taxi ($6–10/person each way), reef 30 yds offshore. Backup: Gumbalimba Park (monkeys + zipline) if rain reshapes the day.
- **Costa Maya** — the pool complex inside the gated port, free with cruise cards; five minutes from re-boarding for nap runs.
- **Cozumel** — Chankanaab Beach Adventure Park (~$50) for kid-calm snorkeling; Palancar (~$50 indep) for confident swimmers on a calm day.
- **Ocean Cay** — **Seakers Family Cove** first (protected shallow zones); Beach Shack lunch 11–4; Marine Conservation Center free tour — the best half-hour of ocean stewardship the kids will get all week.

### The lighthouse show at Ocean Cay

**Runs only on overnight or late-departure calls — and this sailing's published window (7 AM – 6 PM) is a daytime call, so the full show most likely does not run.** Verify in the app once aboard; if MSC extends the call, Lighthouse Bay is the seating area. Tell the kids the honest version before they see it on a poster.

### Photo spots worth knowing

- **World Promenade Deck 8 aft sailaway** — wake beneath the Y-stern, downtown Miami fading in December's early dusk.
- **The Galleria LED ceiling** — scene changes through the day.
- **Roatán West Bay Beach** — white sand, turquoise, reef offshore (rain-washed light can be the best of the week).
- **Ocean Cay striped lighthouse** at golden hour — Lighthouse Bay beach has the angle.
- **Gala night, everyone dressed** — the Photo Package earns its keep here.
