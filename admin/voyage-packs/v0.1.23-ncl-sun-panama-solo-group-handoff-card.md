## What the at-home contact does if a check-in is missed

1. **Don't panic.** A missed message isn't, by itself, a sign of trouble.
2. **Wait for the next check-in,** then try phone and messages.
3. **Call Tina.** She's the group's host and is on the ship.
4. **Call NCL's ship number** above. NCL says it's paid by card.
5. **For a real emergency, call the State Department** at +1 (888) 407-4747. US embassy sites say Americans who need help can call it 24/7.
6. **To see where the ship is,** use cruisinginthewake.com/tools/ship-tracker.html.

*Numbers checked October 2026 on each US embassy's own site, NCL's FAQ and the UK government's travel advice. Check them again before you go. Soli Deo Gloria.*
