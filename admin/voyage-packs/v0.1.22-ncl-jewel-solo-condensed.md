## Getting to the ship and getting home

### Sunday arrival, Monday boarding

**Sunday, November 28, 2027: fly in.** It's the Sunday after Thanksgiving, so book early.

**Monday, November 29: boarding.**

- **Terminal:** PortMiami lists Terminal B, "The Pearl of Miami," as NCL's terminal. Your eDocs name the terminal for your sailing.
- **Parking:** Garage B, 1756 N. Cruise Blvd., $35 a day (PortMiami, effective January 2025). No debit cards.
- **Arrival:** if you've checked in online, arrive no later than one hour before the boarding time you chose. Be aboard by 3:00 PM.
- **Carry-on:** passport, medications, swimsuit, change of clothes, charger.

### Thursday, December 9: home

- The ship is due in at 7:00 AM.
- Keep your passport, medications and a change of clothes with you.

## Areas of interest during the cruise

### On the ship

- **Included restaurants:** Azura and Tsar's Palace (main dining), the [Garden Café](/restaurants/ncl/garden-cafe.html) buffet, The Great Outdoors, Topsiders, and [O'Sheehan's](/restaurants/ncl/osheehans.html), which NCL lists as open 24 hours.
- **Specialty restaurants:** Cagney's, Moderno, La Cucina, Le Bistro, Teppanyaki, Chin Chin and Sushi. $40 to $60 cover, plus 20%, beyond your Free at Sea meals.
- **Free at Sea** (bookings from November 5, 2025): open bar for 21 and over, four specialty meals, 150 Wi-Fi minutes, and a $50 excursion credit per port for the first guest. NCL charges a daily service charge on the drink package. Ask for the amount on your booking.
- **Paid extras:** Vibe Beach Club (adults only) and the spa's Thermal Suite.
- **No Studio cabins or Studio Lounge on Jewel,** but she has **Solo Inside, Oceanview and Balcony** cabins, where NCL says "double occupancy rates don't apply."

### Port-day picks (one each)

- **Great Stirrup Cay:** a beach and a lounger, free.
- **Cabo Rojo:** an NCL excursion to Bahía de las Águilas, which NCL calls "the country's most beautiful beach."
- **Aruba:** an afternoon and a sunset. Ship leaves at 8:00 PM.
- **Curaçao:** Willemstad's waterfront and the Queen Emma Bridge.
- **Falmouth:** a walk through the old town by the pier.
- **Grand Cayman:** go ashore early and come back early. It's a tender port.
