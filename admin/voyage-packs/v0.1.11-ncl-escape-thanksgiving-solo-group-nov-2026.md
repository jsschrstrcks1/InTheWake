## Welcome {#welcome}

> Made for the solo travelers in Pam's Thanksgiving group, and for anyone else on this sailing who wants one plain read on the week: the ship, the four stops, Thanksgiving Day, and what to check before you go.

This pack is about one cruise: **[Norwegian Escape](/ships/norwegian/norwegian-escape.html)**, Norwegian Cruise Line, sailing 7 nights round trip from **Port Canaveral, Friday November 20 to Friday November 27, 2026**. NCL calls it a "7-Day Eastern Caribbean from Orlando (Port Canaveral)." She calls at **Puerto Plata (Amber Cove), St. Thomas, Tortola and Great Stirrup Cay**, with two sea days. **Thanksgiving Day, Thursday November 26, is your day at Great Stirrup Cay**, and the evening is back on board.

It's a **hosted solo cruisers group** led by **Pam Walters**. The group talks in the [Thanksgiving Group Cruise Facebook chat](https://www.facebook.com/share/1DuHTTtgdc/). Join it before you sail: that's where Pam posts the sailaway meeting spot, the group dinner plans and the daily where-are-we-meeting notes. Once you're aboard, Pam is the one to find in person. For booking questions, and what your group rate includes, ask your travel agent; for this group that's [Tina Maulsby](/authors/tina-maulsby.html) of Maulsby Travel.

**Your ship comes out of a refresh just before you sail.** NCL's own Escape page says the ship is "getting an exciting new refresh coming late October": an expanded Vibe Beach Club, a new restaurant called Palomar, a Starbucks in the Atrium and a pickleball court. Cruise Industry News reported in October 2026 that she'd spend 16 days in a European shipyard, then cross back to start her Caribbean season from Port Canaveral in mid-November. Your sailing is the second of those Caribbean weeks, so some of what's new will still be new to the crew too. Check the app for what's open.

### A note on what's first-hand here and what isn't

I haven't sailed Norwegian Escape. Pam and Tina know this group and this kind of cruise first-hand; I don't, so where they tell you something different, go with them. This pack comes from NCL's own pages (its schedule for this exact sailing, its ship page, its "What's On Board" page, its deck plans, its FAQ and its press releases), Port Canaveral's own site, and government pages for each stop. Where only a travel site or a newspaper had the answer, the pack says so. Where nobody had it, the pack says that too. The fact-check file behind this pack lists what was checked and where.

**Prices and policies here were checked in October 2026**, five weeks before you sail. NCL can change them. Where this pack and the NCL app disagree, go with the app.

It is calm by design. No urgency, no upsell, no countdown timer. The site that produced this pack is independent: no cruise-line affiliations, no shore-excursion commissions, no display ads. (See our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure.html).)

**A note on the season.** NOAA's National Hurricane Center says the Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30, so your whole week falls inside it, right at the end. NOAA adds that tropical cyclone activity "sometimes occurs before and after these dates." Travel insurance still makes sense.

**Includes an Emergency Contacts section** with a fillable handoff card to leave with one trusted person at home. The companion app keeps the card on your phone, and it works offline once it's loaded.

If this is your first time sailing solo, the [solo cruising stories on this site](/solo.html) are worth a read before you go.

## Section 1 — Your Ship: Norwegian Escape {#section-1}

### The first of her class, just refreshed

[Norwegian Escape](/ships/norwegian/norwegian-escape.html) was built by Meyer Werft in Papenburg, Germany. The shipyard's release says NCL took delivery on October 22, 2015, at a ceremony in Bremerhaven, "after a building period of only 18 months," and calls her "the first ship in the company's new Breakaway Plus Class." She was christened at PortMiami on November 9, 2015, with the musician Pitbull as her godfather, as NCL announced at the time (Cruise Industry News). Her sisters in the class are [Norwegian Joy](/ships/norwegian/norwegian-joy.html), [Norwegian Bliss](/ships/norwegian/norwegian-bliss.html) and [Norwegian Encore](/ships/norwegian/norwegian-encore.html).

Gross tonnage, 164,998 for her, isn't a weight. The International Maritime Organization says it "is a function of the moulded volume of all enclosed spaces of the ship." Divide it by NCL's guest count and you get about 39 gross tons per guest. The [Ship Size Atlas](/tools/ship-size-atlas.html) lines her up against other ships.

**The late-October 2026 refresh.** NCL's ship page lists four changes: Vibe Beach Club "is expanding," "Palomar is joining Norwegian Escape's lineup of world-class restaurants," a "new Starbucks location in the Atrium," and a pickleball court. NCL hasn't said where Palomar goes or whether it replaces anything, and its "What's On Board" page hadn't caught up when this was written. Check the app.

### What's on board

These come from NCL's "What's On Board" page for Escape, read October 2026, before the refresh. Check the app for the current line-up.

- **Included restaurants:** [The Manhattan Room](/restaurants/ncl/the-manhattan-room.html) (Deck 7), [Taste](/restaurants/ncl/taste.html) and [Savor](/restaurants/ncl/savor.html) (Deck 6), the [Garden Café](/restaurants/ncl/garden-cafe.html) buffet (Deck 16), the [American Diner](/restaurants/ncl/american-diner.html) (Deck 17), and [O'Sheehan's Neighborhood Bar & Grill](/restaurants/ncl/osheehans.html) (Deck 7).
- **Specialty restaurants (extra):** [Cagney's Steakhouse](/restaurants/ncl/cagneys-steakhouse.html), [Food Republic](/restaurants/ncl/food-republic.html), [La Cucina](/restaurants/ncl/la-cucina.html), [Bayamo by Ocean Blue](/restaurants/ncl/bayamo.html), [Moderno Churrascaria](/restaurants/ncl/moderno-churrascaria.html) and [Pincho Tapas Bar](/restaurants/ncl/pincho-tapas-bar.html) on Deck 8, and [Teppanyaki](/restaurants/ncl/teppanyaki.html) and [Le Bistro](/restaurants/ncl/le-bistro.html) on Deck 6. NCL's page gives a cover charge of $40 to $60 a person, and its gratuity FAQ adds a 20% charge to specialty dining. [Palomar](/restaurants/ncl/palomar.html) joins after the refresh.
- **Bars:** among others, [Spice H2O](/restaurants/ncl/spice-h2o.html) (Deck 17, adults only), the [Sugarcane Mojito Bar](/restaurants/ncl/sugarcane-mojito-bar.html), [The Cellars Wine Bar](/restaurants/ncl/the-cellars-wine-bar.html), The District Brew House and Tobacco Road, all on Deck 8.
- **Shows:** the Escape Theater, [Headliners Comedy Club](/restaurants/ncl/headliners-comedy-club.html) and The Supper Club. NCL's page lists shows including The Choir of Man and Howl at the Moon. It doesn't publish showtimes per sailing, so check the app.
- **Outdoors:** the Ropes Course (Deck 18), the Aqua Park (Deck 16) with the Aqua Racer and Free Fall slides (Deck 20), mini golf (Deck 18), and **The Waterfront**, the outdoor promenade on Deck 8.
- **Spa and fitness:** the Mandara Spa and the Pulse Fitness Center (Deck 16). The spa's **Thermal Suite** needs its own paid pass, and NCL says guests must be 18 or older for spa services.
- **[Vibe Beach Club](/restaurants/ncl/vibe-beach-club.html)** on Deck 19 is adults only and needs a paid pass. It's one of the spaces NCL is expanding.

### Cabin choice: sailing solo

Escape is one of the NCL ships with **Studio** cabins and the **[Studio Lounge](/restaurants/ncl/studio-lounge.html)**. NCL's page lists the Studio Lounge on Deck 11 and says it's "exclusive to Studio guests." NCL's solo page lists complimentary coffee, espresso and snacks there, and keycard access. On Escape's deck plans, NCL's Solo Studio is listed at 99 square feet.

Escape's deck plans also list **Solo Inside, Solo Oceanview and Solo Balcony** cabins, each "exclusively for one guest," and each with access to the Studio Lounge. So whatever solo cabin you're in, the lounge is yours too.

Pam's group flyer priced three cabins: **Solo Studio $1,249**, **Ocean View $1,569** and **Balcony $1,839**. Tina has the current numbers and what's bundled with them.

The [stateroom check](/stateroom-check.html) can tell you about a specific cabin number. On most Norwegian ships its cabin list is still partial, and it says so.

### Free at Sea

For bookings made from November 5, 2025, NCL's package is called **Free at Sea**. Per NCL's Free at Sea page, read October 2026, it includes:

- An **open bar** for guests 21 and over, on board and at Great Stirrup Cay
- **Specialty dining**: NCL's table gives **three meals on a 7- or 8-night cruise**
- **Wi-Fi**: 150 minutes
- A **$50 shore excursion credit** at each port, for the first guest in the cabin

The open bar isn't free of charges. NCL's own Free at Sea flyer (December 2025) says: "Gratuities are $28.50/per person/per day for all Guests 21+. Please note this is the pre-cruise price. The onboard price will vary." For seven nights, that's **about $200**, on top of the daily service charge. A group booking can bundle things differently, so **check your own confirmation with Tina**. The [drink calculator](/drink-calculator.html?line=ncl) uses the same $28.50.

### Connectivity

NCL's Wi-Fi runs on Starlink. Free at Sea includes 150 minutes. Beyond that, NCL's Free at Sea page lists the **Voyage Wi-Fi Pass at $29.99** and the **Streaming Voyage Wi-Fi Pass at $39.99**, each per cabin per day, plus $14.99 a day for each extra device (October 2026). The [internet at sea guide](/internet-at-sea.html) explains how ship Wi-Fi works.

## Section 2 — Day by Day {#section-2}

Each port day has the times from NCL's schedule, the all-aboard time, and a few things to know. The port pages on this site go deeper.

> **This is not your plan.** What follows is one possible shape of the week, not a schedule to
> keep. Only a few things on it are actually fixed: the muster drill, sailaway, the ship's arrival
> and all-aboard times in each port, and anything you have already booked. Everything else is a
> suggestion you are free to ignore, reorder, or sleep through. A day spent entirely on your balcony
> is not a day wasted, and nothing here is a commitment you have made to anyone.

### Day 1 — Friday, November 20 — Port Canaveral (embarkation)

**Ship leaves:** 4:00 PM. **Be on board by 3:00 PM.**

- **The terminal.** NCL's Port Canaveral page says: "NCL typically sails out of Cruise Terminal 5 (CT5) and Cruise Terminal 6 (CT6), but this is subject to change." **Your eDocs name yours.**
- **Parking.** Port Canaveral's own page says parking is "$20.00 per day plus tax," counting both the day you arrive and the day you leave. It's paid when you drive in, by credit card only (no cash), and the port says, "We do not take parking reservations." Seven nights is eight days of parking.
- **Arrival time.** NCL asks guests who've checked in online to arrive at the terminal no later than one hour before their chosen boarding time.
- **Carry-on.** Keep your ID documents, medications, a swimsuit and a change of clothes with you. NCL asks you to carry a small bag with what you need on the first day "while your luggage is delivered" to your cabin.
- **Sailaway with the group.** Pam posts the meeting spot in the Facebook chat. Spice H2O at the stern (adults only) and The Waterfront on Deck 8 are both outdoors.

**Flying in.** Fly in the day before, Thursday November 19, so a late flight doesn't cost you the cruise.

More on the [Port Canaveral port page](/ports/port-canaveral.html).

### Day 2 — Saturday, November 21 — At sea

The first sea day. Book your specialty dinners and any shore excursions you still want, find the Studio Lounge if you're in a solo cabin, and sleep in if you like. The Ropes Course and the Aqua Park are both up top.

### Day 3 — Sunday, November 22 — Puerto Plata (Amber Cove), Dominican Republic {#day-puerto-plata}

**In port:** 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. **All aboard 3:00 PM.**

- **The pier.** NCL's port page lists the Amber Cove Cruise Terminal and says the ship will dock. Amber Cove's own site puts it on the Bay of Maimón, on the Dominican Republic's north coast.
- **What to do.** NCL sells Shore Excursions from here, and this site's [Amber Cove page](/ports/amber-cove.html) has more.
- **Money.** NCL says the local currency is the Dominican peso. Ask before you pay in dollars.
- **Emergency number:** 911. The UK government notes that 911 "does not have full coverage across the country."
- **Travel advisory.** The US State Department rates the Dominican Republic Level 2, "exercise increased caution" (June 2025).

### Day 4 — Monday, November 23 — St. Thomas, US Virgin Islands {#day-st-thomas}

**In port:** 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM. **All aboard 6:00 PM.**

A late-morning arrival and an evening departure, so you have the afternoon.

- **Which dock.** Charlotte Amalie has two cruise docks. The Virgin Islands Port Authority runs Crown Bay; the West Indian Company runs the dock at Havensight. The Port Authority's Crown Bay schedule for this period doesn't list Escape on November 23, which points to Havensight, but NCL doesn't say. **Check the app.**
- **It's US territory.** NCL lists the currency as the US dollar. The emergency number is 911.
- **Magens Bay.** The beach is run by the Magens Bay Authority, which charges visitors an entry fee. VI Consortium reported that from October 1, 2023, the fee is $7 for non-residents over 12. Check before you go.
- More on the [St. Thomas port page](/ports/st-thomas.html).

### Day 5 — Tuesday, November 24 — Tortola, British Virgin Islands {#day-tortola}

**In port:** 6:45 AM to 2:00 PM. **All aboard 1:00 PM.**

The shortest day of the week, and the earliest. Plan a morning, not a day.

- **Dock or tender.** NCL's Tortola page lists Road Town Harbor and says the ship "will DOCK or ANCHOR OFF SHORE at this port." If she anchors, you go ashore by tender. **The app will say that morning**, and the last tender back leaves before all-aboard time.
- **Money.** NCL lists the currency as the US dollar.
- **Emergency number:** 911 for ambulance, fire and police. The UK government's travel advice also gives 767 for Virgin Islands Search and Rescue, for emergencies at sea.
- **US help.** The BVI has no US embassy. The CIA World Factbook names the US Embassy in Bridgetown, Barbados as the contact, and that embassy's own site gives its after-hours line as 1-246-227-4000.
- **Travel advisory.** The US State Department rates the BVI Level 1, "exercise normal precautions" (May 2026).
- More on the [Tortola port page](/ports/tortola.html).

### Day 6 — Wednesday, November 25 — At sea

The second sea day, and the day before Thanksgiving. A good day for the Thermal Suite, a show, or a long lunch, and a good night to call home if tomorrow will be hard.

### Day 7 — Thursday, November 26 — Great Stirrup Cay, Bahamas (Thanksgiving Day) {#day-great-stirrup-cay}

**In port:** 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. **All aboard 5:00 PM.**

This is NCL's own island in the Bahamas, and you're here for Thanksgiving.

- **The pier.** NCL says a multi-ship pier debuted in December 2025. When it opened, NCL said all ships visiting the island will benefit from it, with "guests no longer being required to tender from ship to shore" (quoted by Cruise Industry News). In September 2026 Cruise Industry News reported that "the new pier opened in August 2026." So expect to walk ashore, and **check the app** the day before.
- **What's free:** the beaches, loungers and umbrellas, and the Splash Harbor splash pad. The Free at Sea open bar applies on the island.
- **What costs extra:** the **Great Tides Waterpark**, which opened in September 2026, was "from $89" on NCL's excursion page in October 2026. Also cabanas, the Vibe Shore Club day pass, the zipline, and snorkel and paddleboard rentals.
- **Money.** NCL's ships use US dollars, charged to your shipboard account. NCL lists your key card among the things to bring to the waterpark.
- **Thanksgiving evening** is back on board. See [Section 12](#section-12). If you'd like a shower and a rest before dinner, leave the island early; all aboard is 5:00 PM.

More on the [Great Stirrup Cay port page](/ports/great-stirrup-cay.html).

### Day 8 — Friday, November 27 — Port Canaveral (debarkation)

The ship is due in at 7:00 AM. Keep your ID, medications and a change of clothes with you. Since she isn't due until 7:00 AM, **don't book an early-morning flight.** It's also Black Friday, so leave extra time on the roads.

## Section 4 — Packing List {#section-4}

This list fits **late November in the Caribbean**: warm days, a chance of showers, four port days and two sea days. The [packing lists](/packing-lists.html) page has more.

### Documents and money

- **Passport** (or the birth certificate and photo ID NCL allows)
- A copy of your passport, kept somewhere else
- Your insurance policy and its emergency number, printed
- Two cards, kept in different places
- Some small US bills for taxis and tips ashore

### Carry-on

- All medications, in their original bottles
- Phone, charger, power bank
- Refillable water bottle
- Swimsuit and a change of clothes

### Daytime

- Swimsuits and a cover-up
- Light shirts and shorts
- A light sweater for the air conditioning
- A light rain jacket
- Sunglasses, hat, reef-safe sunscreen

### Evenings

NCL's restaurant dress code: "shirts and footwear required," and "swimwear should be saved for the pool." Pack whatever makes you comfortable at dinner within that. If you'd like to dress up a little for Thanksgiving dinner, bring one thing for it.

### Footwear

- Walking shoes for Charlotte Amalie and Road Town
- Water shoes for the beaches
- Sandals

### Wine

NCL lets you carry on bottles of wine and champagne, but not box wine or other alcohol. Its corkage fee is $15 per 750 ml bottle, waived if you have a drink package.

If they don't hear from you by the check-in time:

1. **Don't panic.** A missed message isn't, by itself, a sign of trouble.
2. **Wait for the next check-in.** Then try your phone and messages.
3. **Ask Tina or Pam.** Tina is the group's booking agent; Pam is on the ship and in the Facebook chat.
4. **Call NCL's ship number** above. NCL says it's paid by card.
5. **If it's serious,** call the State Department: +1 (888) 407-4747. US embassy sites say Americans who need help can call it 24/7.
6. **To see where the ship is,** use the [ship tracker](/tools/ship-tracker.html).

The page on [reaching someone at sea](/reaching-someone-at-sea.html) explains how all this works.

**Where these numbers come from.** The State Department numbers and the Santo Domingo and Nassau lines are from each US embassy's own website; the Bridgetown line is from that embassy's contact page, and the CIA World Factbook names it as the contact for the BVI. NCL's ship number is from NCL's FAQ. The local emergency numbers are from the UK government's travel advice. Tina's contact details are from the group's booking information. Check them again before you go.

## Section 7 — Weather and Seasickness {#section-7}

### Weather

NOAA publishes official climate normals for US weather stations, worked out from 1991 to 2020. Two of your stops have one. These are the averages for November 20 to 27, not a forecast. The companion app shows a real forecast as the dates get close.

| Stop | NOAA station | Typical high | Typical low | Chance of rain on a given day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Port Canaveral | Melbourne airport, about 20 miles south | 76°F | 59°F | 26% |
| St. Thomas | St. Thomas airport | 86°F | 76°F | 58% |

"Chance of rain" is NOAA's figure for 0.01 inch or more in a day, so a passing shower counts. Read it as how often you might see rain, not how often a day is spoiled.

**Puerto Plata, Tortola and Great Stirrup Cay** have no NOAA normals, and the weather service this site uses for averages was over its daily limit when this pack was checked, so this pack leaves them blank rather than guess. Tortola sits about 20 miles east of St. Thomas. The companion app's forecast covers all five stops from about two weeks out.

**Hurricanes.** NOAA says the season ends November 30, three days after you're home. NCL's own terms say "itineraries are subject to change without notice."

### Seasickness

Nobody can promise how your body will feel at sea. If you're prone to seasickness:

- **Think about where your cabin is.** A ship pitches and rolls around its middle, so a cabin low and near the middle moves the least.
- **Ask your doctor or pharmacist** about meclizine, dimenhydrinate or a prescription scopolamine patch, which the CDC names, and try anything new at home first.
- **Ginger and acupressure bands:** the CDC says ginger has "only weak and contradictory supporting evidence," and that bands are "no more effective than placebo" in laboratory trials.
- **Fresh air and a view of the horizon:** the CDC says a visible horizon gives "some protection" on ships, and suggests fresh air as a distraction.
- **The ship's medical center** can treat seasickness. It bills you. Medicare's own guide says it "doesn't cover health care services you get when the ship is more than 6 hours away from a U.S. port," so check what your plan covers.

## Section 12 — Thanksgiving Aboard {#section-12}

The holiday is the reason this group exists, so it gets its own section.

### What NCL says

NCL's Thanksgiving page promises "festive menus without the stress." That's as specific as it gets. NCL doesn't publish a Thanksgiving menu, which restaurants serve it, or the times, so this pack doesn't guess. **Check the daily schedule in the app that morning.** The specialty restaurants are there if you'd rather not have the traditional meal; if you want one that night, book it early.

### The group

Pam's plan for the holiday dinner, the time and the place, will be in the Facebook chat.

### The 5 PM moment

For a lot of solo Thanksgiving cruisers, the hardest minute of the day comes around 4:30 or 5:00 PM ship time. Back home, the family is sitting down. The grandkids are at the table. The chair you usually sit in is filled by someone else, or it's empty. You can feel that from a ship in the Bahamas.

This is the part nobody briefs you for, and this pack won't pretend it doesn't happen.

Some options for that hour:

- **Go to Pam's group dinner.** Others at that table may be feeling the same thing at the same hour. A full table is the Thanksgiving this cruise can give you.
- **Step away if you need to.** Tears at the rail on Thanksgiving aren't weakness. Take ten minutes. Call home on the ship's Wi-Fi if you can, send a message if you can't. Then come down to dinner.
- **Skip dinner entirely if that's the answer.** The cabin door locks and the pillow is yours. If you came so you wouldn't have to perform a celebration, that's a fair way to spend this trip too.

You don't have to explain anything to anyone.
